Your working tree and the checked-out branch stay as they are.

After this first sync, Branch Deck syncs whenever it opens the repository, after each change it makes, and when you come back to the window after a commit, a fetch or a push elsewhere (Auto-sync on Focus, in the sync settings next to Sync).
