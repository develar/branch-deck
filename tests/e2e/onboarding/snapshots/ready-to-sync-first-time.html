Ready to Sync

Sync groups the commits of your branch by the prefix of their subject, like (auth) Add login form or ABC-123 Fix login bug , and creates a local branch user-name/virtual/ for each group. Commits without a prefix are listed as unassigned.

Your working tree and the checked-out branch stay as they are.

After this first sync, Branch Deck syncs whenever it opens the repository and after each change it makes. To sync whenever you come back to the window, turn on Auto-sync on Focus in the sync settings next to Sync.
