Choose your branch prefix

Your branches are named /virtual/ : a personal prefix, like your username, keeps them apart from the branches of others.

Branches will be named test/virtual/ . You can change it later in Settings.

Or set it in Git for all repositories: 

                git config --global branchdeck.branchPrefix your-name

Once it is set, you can sync your branches and start organizing your commits.
