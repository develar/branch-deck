# Proposal

## Why

The first Plan of Landing promised more than it did and read badly in places: "Land Together · Safe Push Whole Stack"
only restacked and left the user to find the start; a press jumped to the branches; a few units stretched into wide
cards and a frame across the island; cards of one row stretched to the tallest; at the narrowest islands the last wave
was cut off with no sign that it scrolls; the price in the popover wrapped one risk alone.

## What Changes

- A Land Together of a stack that forks says so in its price ("1 robot instead of 2, the whole stack"), and its toast
  offers "Safe Push Whole Stack" beside Undo, as Rust offers that action (disabled while its stack branch is not built);
  a press in the Plan keeps the Plan.
- The columns are 10 to 18rem wide, the rest of the island empty; past the room of the island the picture scrolls
  across with a fade at the side that has more, and the focus scrolls into view clear of it.
- Cards stand at the top of their row; the curves meet the cards at their headers; the hover of an edge fades every
  card, curve and frame off it, as the edge a Status cell shows.
- The price is one muted sentence that wraps between words, in the plan and in the popover.

Capabilities: `branch/landing-plan`.
