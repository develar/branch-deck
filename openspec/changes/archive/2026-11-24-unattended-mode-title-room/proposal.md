# Proposal

## Why

The mark of Unattended Mode beside Settings takes room that a custom title bar of Windows and Linux lacks at the
minimum window width while the mode is on: the repository selector and the switcher of the views overlapped at 960 px.

## What Changes

- While the mode is on, under a custom title bar the time of the last sync shows from 992 px of the header
  (`@min-[62rem]`); off, and beside the traffic lights and under the system title bar, from 768 px (`@3xl`) as before.
- The word "Unattended" of the mark shows from 1088 px of the header; the tinted moon alone below.

Capabilities: `repository/update-project`.
