# Proposal

## Why

The fix of the reading chips (2026-11-13-review-reading-chips) made the title of a row give way first, down to its
first words, before the meta. That reversed what Your Turn and Reviews had decided and tested: the title is what the
row is, so the meta gives way before it. A hybrid of a basis the title shrinks to was one more rule for no gain.

## What Changes

- The order a narrow row gives way in is the established one: the meta first, to nothing, then the title, then the
  labels, each cut with an ellipsis; what it leaves to read and its age never shrink. The label chips keep the fix of
  their weights: none loses a fraction of a pixel, and with it an ellipsis, while the title has room.
