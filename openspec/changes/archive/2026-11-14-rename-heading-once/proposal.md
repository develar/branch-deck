# Proposal

## Why

The row of a declaration renamed where no index proves it said the rename twice: "totalOf — renamed from sumOf, not
proven · Renamed sumOf → totalOf · 4 sites". The heading names the rename; the note of the rename in the same row only
needs to add what the heading does not say, its sites.

## What Changes

- `HeadingPart.renamed`: the rename a heading names (`formatUnitHeading` of a unit with `renamed_from`).
- `presentRemark(facts, named)`: a rename the heading names is its sites alone (`RenamedWords.named`, no label where it
  is the first); the name of the note for a screen reader keeps the rename in full. The painter draws it so:
  "totalOf — renamed from sumOf, not proven · 4 sites".
- The card of a name and `brd` word no note: nothing changes there.
- Capability `branch/review-units`: the row says the rename once.
