# Proposal

## Why

A meta with less room than its first part showed a bare "#" without an ellipsis: the weight that made the meta give
way first shrank it to a sliver of its first character.

## What Changes

- The meta of a row (a request, a stack, a returned pull request) is in the room the row has left after its title and
  its chips, never more (`MetaPlace`, a query container): it gives way first, to nothing, without a weight; below the
  room of its first part it is hidden whole, so it shows "#402 · …" or nothing.
