## 1. The history

- [x] 1.1 `fs_walk::changes::changed_since`: one stream a volume for the queries whose marks lie close, each event routed
  by its id and path (`Router`), `HistoryRefusal`, a loop of short runs until `HistoryDone` or the deadline
- [x] 1.2 `pool_disk`: one call for every measure, a late history keeps the saved bytes, a day unchecked walks in full

## 2. The parts

- [x] 2.1 `fs_walk`: the share of a hard link, the frontier of subtrees by their entries and the directories above it,
  `dir_listing`
- [x] 2.2 `pool_disk`: a changed path selects its deepest saved part; a directory above the frontier is read alone, its
  new directories walked, its gone ones dropped
